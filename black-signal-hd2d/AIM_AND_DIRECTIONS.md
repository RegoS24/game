# BLACK SIGNAL v0.7.1
Android landscape. The six enemies now have separately illustrated front and rear movement and frontal attack atlases. Each adds 6 × 12 raster frames (four key poses per row plus optical-flow intermediates), 432 new frames total. Side and death art remains from v0.7.0. The sprite is selected using its actual heading relative to the camera, not the player's screen-side position. Front/back are true separate drawings, not stretched side sprites. There are still only three view sectors, not eight separate direction sets; rear stationary/attack holds a planted rear pose. The new source sheet is relatively small and is upscaled to 192×224 cells, so it cannot be called true HD original artwork. Art direction may differ slightly from the prior designs.
Aiming: drag on the right half in two axes, or use Android gyro (enabled by default, zero sensor input is inert). Crosshair stays in its HUD position while vertical projection and hit selection share the actual pitch. Target hit selection uses projected body bounds.
GYRO buttons: toggle; sensitivity 0.5–2.5×; CENTER resets pitch and recalibrates bias (hold device still); vertical invert; swap landscape axes; horizontal invert. Settings persisted. Sensor integration requires physical-device verification; headless test checks math, not hardware. No 3D character or engine switch.
