# Enemy roster (v0.7.0)
Six illustrated enemy types from the approved BLACK SIGNAL concepts: street raider, security guard, cyber brute, netrunner, cyber hound and hover drone. Each WebP atlas contains five 18-frame sequences (idle, walk/hover, run/strafe, attack and death) at 192×224, 540 frames in total, including flow-interpolated frames. Source was generated art segmented into transparent sprites. One 3/4 angle with mirroring; not independently drawn eight-direction art. Pilot encounter is a linear street with six units, AI pursuit, patrol, distance and cooldown-based attacks, HP, minimap, win and retry. No new engine or 3D character. Physical-device performance and visual smoothness still need testing.
