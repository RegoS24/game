# Rin locomotion v0.7.4
Replaced the three locomotion atlases' generation: a shared passing pose, a distinct lifted-leg half-cycle, passing pose, then an anatomically reflected lower-limb half-cycle. Preserves torso and asymmetric turquoise fur. Tests measure the two sneaker centroids at 25% and 75% of the exported cycle and require opposite signed separation above a minimum; build rejects repeated single-leg motion. Local validation at 384×512: run +67.6/-67.7 px, crouch walk +25.3/-25.3 px, crawl -17.7/+18.1 px. Motion-driven continuous phase; full gait period for running increased from 0.70 to 0.80 seconds. A runtime smoke test checks both quarter-cycle poses and wrap. Existing standing art, Android gyro, opponents and world unchanged. Uses composited and interpolated raster artwork, not skeletal animation or independently illustrated frames. Device visual verification remains required.
