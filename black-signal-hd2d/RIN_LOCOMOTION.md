# Rin locomotion v0.7.3
New illustrated rear-view poses for running, crouch walk and prone crawl, keyed to a shared ground anchor. 48 run, 40 crouch-walk, 40 crawl frames, including optical-flow interpolations and mirrored lower limb phases rather than independent hand-drawn frames. No 3D character. Standing walk and stance transitions retain prior art; the new sheet can differ slightly in garment/pose details. All new animation frames are 384x512. Motion drives gait phase and stops in the correct stance. Sprint disabled while crouched or prone; crouch speed 50%, crawl speed 24% of walking speed. Existing gyro, aiming, enemies and enemy scales preserved. Physical-device quality and frame timing remain unverified.
