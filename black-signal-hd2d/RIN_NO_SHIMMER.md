# BLACK SIGNAL v0.7.6 — reduce sprite shimmer
The 90 Hz interpolation exposed noisy and inconsistent details from sparse poses. Keyframe restoration now uses deterministic alpha-premultiplied cubic resampling plus gentle bilateral noise reduction instead of a hallucinating neural SR pass. Optical flow is spatially regularized and still preserves leg changes. Pack pages gain 8-pixel transparent gutters around every sampled frame, avoiding tile-adjacent bleed. Godot-generated texture imports are switched to lossless + mipmaps; the canvas uses linear mipmapped filtering to reduce shimmering while Rin is minified. Both emulator and headless smoke are build-gated. The 90-Hz nominal sample count remains, but this is *not* a guarantee of true natural motion: the source contains too few distinct painted poses. For genuine stable animation without frame-warping, next replace sprite morphing with a segmented 2D skeletal/cutout rig. Reuse CC0 motion as reference or retargeted bone data; don't paste foreign sprites over Rin or assume licenses permit it.
